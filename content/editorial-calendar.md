# Narristotle — Editorial Calendar

Cadence: two articles per week.
- **Tuesday — The Story Clinic:** a practical storytelling problem and a worked example.
- **Friday — Contrarian Craft / breakdown / Bookshelf:** a contrarian essay, a story breakdown, or a Bookshelf feature.

Every piece must have: a clear takeaway, a concrete example, something the reader can apply the same day, and a "Narristotle's Verdict" or "The Short Answer" summary box. Snark aims at bad writing advice, never at struggling writers. No invented quotations, credentials, or product claims. No consumer prices or drafting-speed benchmarks, ever.

Workflow: Muse drafts → Steve or Matt edits/approves in /studio → publish (single-editor, no multi-person approval) → action lands in the publication history. Scheduled posts can be overridden or unpublished at any time by either editor.
