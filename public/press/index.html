Long bio

Narristotle is the editorial persona and storytelling methodology of AI Global Entertainment, a company co-founded by Matt Allen and Steve Smythe. Allen built the Method — story structure, character transformation, setups and payoffs, dramatic cause and effect. Smythe built the technology that turned it into RoughDraft Maker, where writers bring an idea, messy notes, or a dictated version of their story and shape it into a working rough draft. Narristotle.com is the editorial home of the same thinking: the Gazette’s essays, the Bookshelf’s study notes, and the Method, published so any writer can use them.

The press publishes books made with its own tools. Its featured book is NO CONTACT, a completed horror novella by M. R. Saran, free in PDF, EPUB, and Kindle editions. Narristotle is seeking literary representation, and the outreach is a matter of public record.

Questions, answered

What is Narristotle?
Narristotle is the editorial persona and storytelling methodology of AI Global Entertainment. The marble bust, the tortoiseshell glasses, the claim to have been born in 335 BCE and recompiled in California — that is a character, a playful fiction wrapped around a real methodology. He is not a real person, a historical figure, or a corporate officer. The method, the essays, and the company are real.

Who writes the books?
Books on this site are written with RoughDraft Maker, AI Global Entertainment’s story-building tool, and published by AI Global Entertainment. NO CONTACT, the featured book, is by M. R. Saran, created with AI assistance.

Who publishes them?
AI Global Entertainment publishes them, free to read on narristotle.com.

Is Narristotle seeking representation?
Yes. Representation and rights inquiries go to press@aiglobalentertainment.com. The outreach is public in the outreach record.

What is the press?
narristotle.com is the publication and the press: the Gadfly Gazette (twice weekly), the Bookshelf, the games, and the books. Coverage and interview requests go to press@aiglobalentertainment.com.
