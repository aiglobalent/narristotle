What to send

Seven things. Send what you have — for anything missing, we either fill it in or leave it out, and we show you which before anything publishes.

  1. 1 · Manuscript

    Your book's text. Your original file is best; a PDF or EPUB also works.

  2. 2 · Cover art

    Your front cover. A separate high-resolution file is best; a cover embedded in your PDF or EPUB also works.

  3. 3 · Title + author name

    As they should appear — including a pseudonym if you write under one.

  4. 4 · Back-cover blurb

    The paragraph that sells the book.

  5. 5 · About the author

    A short bio, in your own words.

  6. 6 · Genre + audience

    What shelf it goes on.

  7. 7 · Rights confirmation

    That you own or hold the right to publish this book here.

Your book stays yours

  • You keep the copyright and all rights. Nothing here transfers, assigns, or sells any right in your book to us.
  • Non-exclusive, revocable. You can publish the same book anywhere else, any time — and you can ask us to take it down at any time.
  • We never claim copyright, exclusivity, derivatives, film or audio rights, the right to sell or sublicense your book, or any use of your manuscript to train AI systems.
  • Nothing publishes without your OK. You approve the manifest first, and nothing goes live until you do.
