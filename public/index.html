Who is Narristotle?

Narristotle is a fictional story-teacher created by AI Global Entertainment — not a real person, not a historical figure, not a corporate officer. His job is to show how stories work: structure, transformation, setups and payoffs, cause and effect.

He teaches through the Gazette, the games, and the tools. The method is Matt Allen’s storytelling methodology; the technology and product development are Steve Smythe’s.
