What Is a Ramble Book? You Ramble. It Becomes a Book.

A Ramble Book is a book made from a ramble. You speak your story — a memory, a joke, a bedtime idea, a business idea, a life story — and RambleBook turns it into a real, illustrated book, ready to read and ready to give. Just like you Google something, you make a Ramble Book.

You ramble. About your kid, your best friend, a business, a resume, a business card that should have been a book, an autobiography, a documentary, a joke only your family gets. You don’t have to know how to write a book. RambleBook figures out what kind of book your ramble wants to be, and makes it. Later, that means more than the first two books: winter in Paris, summer in Thailand, and any other book your ramble points to. The engine is built to feed anything — if you can ramble it, RambleBook can figure out the book for it.
