This is you, right now

You brought home a puppy. Tonight is the first night. The crate is in the bedroom, the puppy is crying, and you are awake at 2 a.m. wondering if you are doing any of this right. By morning there is a puddle by the door, a chewed shoe, and a list of questions for the vet. Google gives you ten answers that disagree with each other. That is the first week. It gets better, but only if the first month goes right.

The first month goes like this

Week one, the puppy learns where to sleep and where to go. Week two, the routine starts to stick. Week three, the puppy knows your voice and comes when you call. Week four, the puppy sleeps through most of the night. And in the middle of all of it, the window for meeting the world is open, then it closes. Your puppy is eight to twelve weeks old right now. That window runs to about fourteen weeks. The month you are in is the month that sets the dog you will live with for years.
